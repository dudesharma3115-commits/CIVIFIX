Terms of Use

Using CivicFix

Use CivicFix to report real civic problems in your area. Give accurate details and photos of the issue itself. Do not upload photos of people's faces or private property interiors, or anything unlawful.

Not for emergencies

CivicFix is not an emergency service. For fire, medical or police emergencies call 112. For a live electrical hazard, also contact your local electricity office directly.

Misuse

False, abusive or repeated spam reports may be rejected and accounts suspended. Administrators can reject reports that are duplicates or outside civic scope.

No guarantee of resolution
