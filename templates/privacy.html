Privacy Policy

What we collect

Your name, email, district and password (stored only as a secure hash). When you report an issue: its description, category, area, optional landmark, a map location if you pin one, and any photo you upload. Photos are re-processed on upload to remove hidden location (EXIF) data. We also keep security logs (sign-ins, actions on cases, IP address).

Why we use it

To verify your account, route your report to the correct government department, notify you of progress, prevent misuse, and report anonymous statistics.

Who can see it

Only the people handling your case: your district administrators and the officers of the department your report is forwarded to. Other citizens see approved issues as anonymous map pins, with no names and no photos.

How long we keep it

Reports are kept as a public-interest record of civic issues. [Set your retention period, for example 3 years after resolution.] Security logs are kept for [12 months].

Your rights

In My account you can download your data and delete your account. Deleting removes your name, email and notifications; past reports stay on record without your identity. For other requests, write to {{ contact_email }}.

Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, private photo storage, rate limiting and an audit log of administrative actions.
